HARBOR & HIVE · DEEP DIVE 06

Analytics & Control Tower

Use KPIs to find the transaction, exception, owner and next action—not just to decorate a dashboard.

What a useful KPI should answer

12Open exceptions → which records and owners?
8Nexus watch jurisdictions → how close to threshold?
96%Evidence completeness → what is missing?
98.7%Reconciliation match → where is the variance?

Drill-down design

Executive view
Risk, deadlines, material exceptions and operating health.
Tax view
Jurisdiction exposure, taxability issues, exemptions and filing status.
Accounting view
Accruals, journal status, GL variances and payment differences.
Data-quality view
Duplicates, missing fields, mapping failures and stale-source conditions.
Case view
Owner, age, severity, evidence and resolution history.
Source lineage
Trace a dashboard number back to the records that produced it.

The principle

A dashboard should shorten the path from “something looks wrong” to “here is the exact record, reason and next action.”
