Data Intake & Normalization

Turn inconsistent source records into governed canonical transactions without erasing where the data came from.

The business problem

The same transaction can appear differently in an Excel workbook, ERP CSV, invoice and email. Names, dates, invoice IDs, jurisdictions and tax fields often do not match exactly. A downstream tax or accounting process cannot safely assume those records are already standardized.

Plain English: normalization means making records speak the same language while preserving the original source.

What the control layer does

Ingest
Receive approved Excel, CSV, invoice, document or connector payloads.
Validate
Check required fields, types, formats and source contracts.
Resolve identities
Recognize ABC Supply, ABC Supply Inc. and ABC SUPPLY as potential matches.
Canonicalize
Create one governed transaction/entity representation for downstream use.
Preserve lineage
Keep the original record, source system and transformation history.
Quarantine exceptions
Stop uncertain records before they silently contaminate tax or accounting results.

Why it matters downstream

Tax determination, GL classification, nexus measurement, reconciliation, return mapping and payment matching become more reliable when they operate on a controlled transaction model rather than five competing versions of the same record.
