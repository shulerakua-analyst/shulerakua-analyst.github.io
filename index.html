PROOF OF WORK

Evidence, not just skill labels.

Each demo shows the business problem, how I structured the solution, what automation can handle and where professional review still matters.

Problem

Work arrives fragmented.

Tax, finance and operational data often live in separate files, systems and workpapers. That makes exceptions hard to see and review slower than it needs to be.

My approach

Structure the path.

I break the work into source data, validation, decision logic, reconciliation, evidence and review so there is a clear next action.

Result

More review, less hunting.

Automation handles repeatable screening and calculations while people spend more time on judgment, documentation and final approval.

DEMO 01 · TAX & NEXUS

Nexus is not just a tax calculation.

The real operational problem is realizing a threshold may have been crossed and then scrambling to determine when registration was required, which transactions were affected, when collection should have started, and what filing exposure already exists.

Problem

Threshold monitoring is easy to miss.

Sales can build across jurisdictions while the underlying exposure remains buried in transaction detail.

What I did

Turned exposure into a visible control.

I organized rolling activity, threshold utilization, status and next action so attention is visible before the work becomes urgent.

Human role

Validate the conclusion.

The control flags the issue; a tax professional confirms the correct rule, dates, exclusions, registrations and remediation.

DEMO 04 · FINANCE & RECONCILIATION

Three views should agree before release.

A practical reconciliation compares the book, the calculation source and the preparation workpaper before the period is treated as complete.

Problem

Differences survive too long.

When workpapers, ledger balances and tax-system totals are reviewed separately, small issues can reach filing or close before anyone sees the mismatch.

What I did

Made agreement a release condition.

The workflow compares the three views, applies a tolerance and routes differences for investigation instead of allowing an assumed match.

Human role

Explain the variance.

Automation finds the gap. Accounting or tax review determines whether it is timing, mapping, source data, calculation or a true adjustment.

DEMO 05 · REVERSE AUDIT / RECOVERY

Start with what was paid and work backward.

Reverse audit asks whether tax was overpaid, whether a credit was missed, whether a payment duplicated, or whether tax should have been challenged.

Problem

Overpayments hide in volume.

Small tax differences, duplicate invoices and missed credits may be individually modest but meaningful in aggregate.

What I did

Created a review funnel.

I compare paid tax with the modeled obligation, surface documented credit candidates and narrow the population that needs human review.

Human role

Prove the recovery.

A reviewer confirms taxability, documentation, statute timing, vendor or jurisdiction procedure, and whether a refund or credit is supportable.
