GUIDED SYSTEM DEMONSTRATION

One synthetic company. Multiple messy sources. One controlled flow.

Apex Manufacturing Group is a fictional multinational organization created for this portfolio. The records deliberately contain duplicate identities, inconsistent formats, missing support and tax variances so the controls have something meaningful to detect.

Portfolio boundary: all company names, invoices, amounts and records in this demo are synthetic. No client or employer data is displayed.
