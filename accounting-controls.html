Core control concepts

Canonical Account Registry
A governed reference chart that maps source accounts into controlled H&H classifications.
Journal Entry Subledger
Stores proposed and approved journal-entry detail rather than leaving calculations disconnected from accounting evidence.
Posting/Status Registry
Tracks whether an entry is proposed, reviewed, approved, posted, reversed or superseded.
Accrual controls
Compare expected liability, prior payments, adjustments and actual liability by period.
Reconciliation
Prove the GL, tax calculation, workpaper and payment tell the same story—or surface the difference.
Evidence chain
Keep the source transaction and reviewer history attached to the accounting outcome.

Why this closes an important gap

A tax system can calculate the right number and still leave the organization with a weak accounting trail. The accounting-control layer makes the tax result operationally usable and traceable.
