DEMO 02 — NEXUS INTELLIGENCE

How I think about nexus

Nexus isn't just a tax calculation. You can calculate the tax perfectly and still have a compliance problem if you discover the obligation after the threshold was crossed. Then you're scrambling to figure out when registration was required, which transactions were affected, what should have been collected, what needs to be filed, and what exposure already exists—yesterday.

I look at nexus as a connected problem: sales + transactions + jurisdiction rules + thresholds + tax calculation + effective dates + registration + filing + evidence. I built this example to show how I would use data to spot an approaching obligation before it turns into a cleanup project.
