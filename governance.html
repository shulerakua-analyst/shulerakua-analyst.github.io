Exceptions & Governance

Make the problems visible, stop bad data from moving downstream and keep the decision history.

The exception lifecycle

DETECTcontrol fails
→
QUARANTINEblock unsafe use
→
ASSIGNroute to owner
→
RESOLVEcorrect + explain
→
RELEASEapproved downstream use

Examples of controlled exceptions

Duplicate transaction
Same economic event appears in multiple files or systems.
Missing jurisdiction
Required location facts are absent or contradictory.
Unsupported exemption
An exemption is claimed but usable support is missing.
Tax variance
Source tax differs from the governed expected result outside tolerance.
Reconciliation mismatch
GL, calculation, return or payment does not agree.
Stale change
A user attempts to overwrite a newer approved version or mapping.

Governance is part of the product, not a footnote

Higher-impact actions can require review, reasons, segregation of duties, version control and immutable history. The aim is not just to automate work—it is to make automated and human decisions inspectable.
