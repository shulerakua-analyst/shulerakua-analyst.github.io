What is governed here

Return Learning & Mapping Registry
Persist approved source-to-form mappings so the next period starts pre-mapped but still validates changes.
Compliance calendar
Track jurisdiction, tax type, filing frequency, due dates and status.
Workpaper package
Tie totals to source records, adjustments, exceptions and supporting evidence.
Change detection
Do not blindly reuse prior mappings when forms, registrations, systems or rules materially change.
Payment readiness
Connect approved liability to payment type, period, account and confirmation.
Filing boundary
Support approved electronic, portal-guided or form-based outputs according to the applicable implementation path.

The point

Compliance should be the controlled output of the upstream data, tax and accounting process—not an isolated workbook rebuilt from scratch every month.
