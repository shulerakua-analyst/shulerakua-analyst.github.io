Tax Analysis & Determination

Translate transaction facts into a reviewable jurisdiction, nexus, taxability and exemption decision path.

What this function is responsible for

The tax-analysis layer consumes governed transaction facts. It interprets controlled facts for tax purposes and records what evidence or rule basis supported the result.

FACTSseller · buyer · product · service · location
→
JURISDICTIONwhere the transaction belongs
→
NEXUSwhether an obligation exists
→
TAXABILITYtaxable · exempt · conditional
→
RESULTcalculation + explanation + evidence

Controls shown in the portfolio

Jurisdiction validation
Reject or review transactions whose location facts are incomplete or contradictory.
Nexus monitoring
Compare governed activity to jurisdiction-specific obligation logic.
Taxability classification
Evaluate product/service treatment rather than assuming every line is identical.
Exemption support
Separate an exemption claim from proof that the exemption is usable.
Rule provenance
Preserve the rule/source/version supporting a determination.
Human review gates
Route uncertain, high-impact or unsupported outcomes for review.

Where the answer goes next

Approved tax results feed accounting classification, accruals, reconciliation, return workpapers, payment readiness and analytics. Exceptions remain visible instead of disappearing into a spreadsheet.
