Key architectural ideas

Vendor-neutral orchestration
The control layer can sit above authoritative source systems and specialist providers rather than forcing one calculation or filing vendor everywhere.
Canonical records
Shared entity, transaction, account and relationship models reduce one-off point-to-point logic.
Registries
Durable configuration such as accounts, mappings, authority status, obligations and rules belongs in governed stores.
Cross-system consistency
Shared control patterns should be reused across U.S. and international processes when the concept applies.
AI behind governance
AI can assist with summaries, explanations and proposed mappings, while deterministic controls and required review govern consequential outputs.
Evidence by design
Source provenance, version, transformation, reviewer action and outcome remain attached to the work.
